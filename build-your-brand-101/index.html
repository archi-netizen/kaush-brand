LEARNING OUTCOMES

01

What you’ll leave with

Six things you will be able to do after the session that you couldn’t do — or weren’t doing correctly — before.

01 Define what your brand actually stands for

Not what you assumed it was. The Before/After workbook makes the gap between instinct and framework visible — that gap is the course.

02 Articulate your positioning clearly

Explain what makes your offer distinct — to clients, to collaborators, to designers, and to yourself — in one sentence that holds up under pressure.

03 Understand visual identity logic

Read a logo, a colour system, a typographic choice — and understand why it works or why it doesn’t. Without needing to be a designer.

04 Develop a consistent voice and tone

Define how your brand sounds — in proposals, on social media, in a cold email — and identify what it should never sound like.

05 Choose the right platforms

Make deliberate decisions about where your brand shows up — and have the discipline to stop wasting time on platforms that don’t fit the strategy.

06 Complete a Brand Brief

Leave the session with a one-page brief you can hand to a designer — or use to guide your own identity work — immediately after the session.

THE METHOD

02

A course with a before & after built in.

5–10 minutes of instinct questions — before any framework.

Before The Session

“What I think my brand is.”

Your assumptions about your audience, visual direction, and voice. No right answers.

After 2H 45M

“What I now know it is.”

The last 15 minutes: every participant shares what changed in one minute. Your before-answer, your after-answer, and the direction between them.

Common Questions

Do I need design skills or software?

This is a thinking and strategy course — not a software tutorial. You do not need to know how to use design tools.

Do I need to be physically in Germany?

No — you can join the session from anywhere. You do need to be tax-resident in Germany and file taxes here. For our tax, VAT, and invoicing compliance, your billing address at checkout must be in Germany.

What if I don’t have a brand yet?

Perfect. The course is designed for people building from scratch. The BEFORE questions are based on instinct, not on an existing identity. Starting from zero is the ideal starting point.

What if I already have a brand and want to refine it?

Also useful — the BEFORE/AFTER structure works for existing brands too. Many participants come with an identity that “feels wrong” and leave knowing exactly why and what to change.

Is the workbook sold separately?

No — the workbook is only available as part of the live course registration. It’s designed to be used alongside the session: the BEFORE tab before we meet, the AFTER tabs during and after.

Will the session be recorded?

No. The session is live only. The full slide deck PDF is sent within 24 hours so you have a complete reference without needing to take notes.

Can I get a refund?

Full refund if requested more than 7 days before the session. If you can’t attend, your registration transfers to the next monthly cohort. See the Terms & Conditions.

Can my employer pay for this?

In principle, yes — an invoice is provided within 24 hours. Whether it qualifies as Fortbildungskosten for your employer depends on your contract and their internal policy.
