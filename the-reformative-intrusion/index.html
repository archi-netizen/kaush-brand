The Reformative Intrusion — in public

Stress-Test · Simulated Public Forum

The dossier, dropped into a simulated forum and argued against. A speculative proposal is only as strong as the objections it survives.

r/SpeculativeUrbanism · carceral architecture · 6.2k reading

This is a simulated forum. The thread, usernames, vote counts, and comments below are authored as a design-research instrument — to stress-test the proposal against the ethical, sociological, logistical, and legal objections it must answer. No real users, accounts, or quotations are represented.

▲1.2k▼

Speculative Designu/k_mateOP· 1d

Keep Nagpur Central Jail in the city centre and turn it into a “Social Hospital” — biometric tethers instead of bars, a porous retail perimeter, and a borrowed-horizon “Shakkei view” as an Article 21 right. Tear it apart.

Speculative counter-model to the planned relocation. Instead of moving the 118-acre jail to the rural outskirts (spatial displacement), it stays at the heart of the Smart City: physical bars replaced by biometric tracking and SBI-linked digital identities; the perimeter becomes a porous membrane where the public buys produce and textiles from a Green Factory run by inmates — as patrons, not voyeurs. Light, Nagpur teak, and Corten jaalis soften the sensory environment to mitigate chronic stress (Ranjish).

I want the strongest objections, not applause. Where does this break?

Pu/panopticon_ghostArchitectural· 14h

A “Porous Panopticon” is still a panopticon. Decentralising one watchtower into 4–6 glass hubs doesn’t remove the gaze — it makes it ambient and constant. The inmate internalises the watcher; glass hubs do that better, not less. You may have upgraded surveillance from a tower you can see to a field you can’t.
