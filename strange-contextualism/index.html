The Research

Architecture as a Collage of Borrowed Elements

Strange Contextualism argues that contemporary architecture is not pure invention — it is a collage of borrowed elements producing specific aesthetic and social effects. The thesis was developed at the Städelschule Architecture Class in Frankfurt and awarded the AIV Best Master Thesis Prize in 2016.

Social Glue
The power of cooking and dining ("The Feast") as the programmatic glue connecting art museum and academic institution.
The Collage
Architectural fragments negotiating the relationship between the Städel Museum and Städelschule — physical and televisual space.
Televisual
Designing for the camera lens — treating the building as a stage for culinary drama that opens institutional space to the public.
Legacy
Foundation of current brand practice: how an organization's "Image" is curated to resonate within its specific cultural and spatial context.
