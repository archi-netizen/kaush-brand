BACKGROUND

The Synthesis

Kaushambi Mate is a design strategist and researcher with a foundation in architecture, working between India and Germany. Her work explores the relationship between built environments, visual culture, and brand identity, with a particular interest in how spaces, images, and design shape cultural identity.

She completed her Bachelor of Architecture in Mumbai before earning her Master’s degree at the Städelschule Architecture Class in Frankfurt am Main. Her master’s thesis was awarded the AIV Best Master’s Thesis Prize in 2016.

She works in English and German, and is a native speaker of Hindi and Marathi — practising across European and Indian contexts.

RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Strange Contextualism

An important starting point for her work is the research project Strange Contextualism. The project explores how images, spaces, and cultural contexts interact, and how meaning is shaped through visibility and perception. This perspective informs her approach to brand design — identity approached with the same attention to structure, materiality, and context that characterises architectural thinking.

RESEARCH & PUBLICATION

Vernacular Branding

Together with her father, she is the co-author of Vernacular Branding: Reading Identity through Design and Visibility (2024–25). The publication examines how design contributes to the formation of cultural identity and connects anthropological perspectives with design practice.

From research to resources: The frameworks in Vernacular Branding inform the Brand Identity Courses. → kaushambimate.com/courses
