The thinking

Pattern, symmetry and the digital gaze.

Mirror Lab Sketches are an early investigation into AI-adjacent image creation. They predate the generative AI wave and mark the path from manual digital tools toward full image synthesis.

The tool

Accessible complexity

Mirror Lab’s simple interface generated kaleidoscopic imagery from photographs at speed. Accessibility as a design philosophy: complexity for everyone.

The trajectory

From Mirror Lab to Midjourney

Each tool in this lineage is a step toward AI-generated imagery, where the archive of human visual culture becomes the training set.

Emotional register

Order out of chaos

The work aims for a strong response: movement, energy and an uncanny sense of order created by mathematical symmetry.
