LEARNING OUTCOMES

01

What you’ll leave with

Six things you will be able to do after the session that you couldn’t do — or weren’t doing correctly — before.

01 Read your Steuerbescheid

Understand every line of your German tax assessment letter — what it means, how to check it, and what to do if it’s wrong.

02 Claim your Werbungskosten

Know exactly which deductions apply to you as an employee — home office, equipment, education, commuting — with the legal paragraph and receipt rule for each.

03 Separate employed and freelance income

Understand Betriebsausgaben, how to track freelance expenses correctly from day one, and how they interact with your employment income.

04 Calculate your tax reserve

Set aside the right amount each month so your annual Nachzahlung doesn’t come as a shock. A formula, not a guess.

05 Navigate Germany as a non-German

Understand double taxation treaties, how your residency status affects your obligations, and how foreign income is treated.

06 Know when to call a Steuerberater

Identify the situations where professional advice is necessary — and know exactly what to bring so you don’t waste your appointment.

01

The German tax system — overview for non-Germans
Who has to file. What the deadlines are. The difference between Einkommensteuererklärung and Lohnsteuer. Why both matter if you’re employed and freelance simultaneously.
25 min

02

Reading your Steuerbescheid
A line-by-line walkthrough of a real tax assessment letter. What each figure means. When to object (Einspruch) and by when.
25 min

03

Werbungskosten — employee deductions in depth
Every deduction available to you as an employee. Home office (Arbeitszimmer), Arbeitsmittel, Fortbildungskosten, Fahrtkosten. Legal basis for each. What receipts you need.
30 min

04

Betriebsausgaben — freelance expenses
What counts as a deductible business expense for your freelance side. How to separate personal from professional. The 10% Gemischte Aufwendungen rule.
30 min

05

The Deductions Cheatsheet — live walkthrough
Working through the 380-row cheatsheet together. Finding your profile. Understanding the limits and the receipts that make or break a deduction.
20 min

+

What to do now — the end-of-year checklist
Practical steps: what to gather, what software to use, when to file, when a Steuerberater is worth it.
10 min

IS THIS FOR ME?

This course is for you if…

  • You are employed in Germany and have any freelance, author, or self-employment income on the side
  • You have received a Steuerbescheid and genuinely didn’t understand what it said
  • You’re not German and the German tax system feels like a bureaucratic black box in a foreign language
  • You suspect you’re missing deductions but don’t know which ones or how to claim them
  • You prefer a live, conversational English explanation over reading German tax guides alone
  • You want to understand the system well enough to have an informed conversation with a Steuerberater

Common Questions

Is this legal or tax advice?

For your individual situation, consult a licensed Steuerberater or Lohnsteuerhilfeverein.

Do I need to be physically in Germany?

No — you can join the session from anywhere. You do need to be tax-resident in Germany and file taxes here. For our tax, VAT, and invoicing compliance, your billing address at checkout must be in Germany.

What if I’m only employed, with no freelance side?

You’ll still benefit — modules 1, 2, and 3 (Steuerbescheid + Werbungskosten) apply fully to employed professionals. However, if you have no freelance income at all, the German Tax Warm Up course (coming soon) may be a better fit.

What software do I need?

Microsoft Excel or compatible (Google Sheets works for the Workbook). Google Meet. No other tools required.

Will the session be recorded?

No. The session is live only. The full slide deck PDF is sent within 24 hours so you have everything without needing notes.
