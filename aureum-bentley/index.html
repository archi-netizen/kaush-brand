Aureum & Bentley


Speculative Brand Luxury Real Estate Brand Novel

A speculative luxury real-estate brand, imagined in collaboration with Bentley to promote Bentley Homes across Europe.

Type Speculative brand world & narrative
Premise Aureum × Bentley · Bentley Homes, Europe
Format Brand novel (flipbook) + mockup gallery
Sector Luxury real estate

Aureum is an imaginary, speculative brand — a luxury real-estate house conceived as a collaboration with Bentley to bring “Bentley Homes” to the European market. It is a brand-world exercise: a complete identity, narrative, and set of mockups, built to test how far a speculative luxury brand can be carried as a coherent story rather than a single logo.

Speculative concept project. Aureum is a fictional brand; this work is an independent design exploration and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or commissioned by Bentley Motors.

The Aureum & Bentley Novel

The brand is presented as a “novel” — a flipbook that carries the world of Aureum from premise to product, in editorial pacing rather than a deck.
