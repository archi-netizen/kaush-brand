A luxury real estate brand identity designed from the spatial experience outward. The logo proportions were stress-tested at signage scale first — bronze letterforms on a concrete wall — then refined for digital use. This is the reverse of how most brand projects work.

METHODOLOGY

Why This Approach?

While traditional graphic design often works on the facade, I develop identities from the ground up. My background at Städelschule taught me to perceive architecture as a medium. Every design project benefits from this combination of technical precision and cultural research.

“Design is not decoration. It is the structure of meaning — the architecture of how we understand.”

Research into Vernacular Branding — exploring how cultural identity and everyday visual language shape commercial brands — informs every identity system built here.
