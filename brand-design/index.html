Methodology

Why this approach?

Traditional graphic design often works on the facade. I develop identities from the ground up. My studies at the Städelschule taught me to see architecture as a medium, and every design project benefits from that mix of technical precision and cultural research.

“Design is not decoration. It is the structure of meaning.”
