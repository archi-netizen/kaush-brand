The River as Genetic Origin

The Main is not merely a border — it is Frankfurt's genetic origin.

Franconofurd: the Ford of the Franks. A 525km axis transformed from Roman military crossing to industrial artery to pedestrianised social space.

The Ford
Frankfurt's name derives from Franconofurd — a shallow limestone crossing that gave Romans and Carolingians a strategic military and trade advantage. The river is the reason the city exists at this coordinate.
The Canalization
1880s: the river transformed into a heavy-duty industrial artery. The Alte Brücke (first mentioned 1222) served as the sole stone crossing for centuries — funnelling all European north-south trade through the city's central market.
Modern Shift
Closure of the Mainkai riverfront (800m) to vehicular traffic in 2019-2020 resulted in a 45% increase in cyclist frequency — a transition from transit space to social space.
