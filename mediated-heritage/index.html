The Research

Mediatecture & Weird Contextualism

The Römer — Frankfurt's 15th-century city hall — becomes a site for Mediatecture. High-lumen laser mapping transforms medieval stepped gables into dynamic carriers of information: distorted photographic fragments of the contemporary metropolis projected onto historic stone.

The Site
The Römerberg's stepped sandstone gables provide the high-contrast geometry required for complex distortion mapping — 15th-century architecture as a digital canvas.
The Content
Frankfurt's intellectual legacy: celebrating 19 Nobel Prize winners through photographic distortions of the city's skyline, nature preserves, and financial district.
Weird Contextualism
A methodology extended from Adil Esset (Städelschule) — contextual inputs deliberately distorted to create a hybrid condition between medieval physical structure and contemporary digital message.
