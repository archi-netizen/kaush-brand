The Feast: Digital Archive

A culinary experiment in food, service and atmosphere.

2016Year
Performance archiveType
Rundgang, Frankfurt am MainVenue
Team of eightMade by

The Feast was a culinary experiment hosted by the Architecture and Aesthetic Practice class and shown at Rundgang, the art academy’s annual open house. An experiential sequence of food and drink in a performative, social-aesthetic setting: a spatially choreographed event where the dialogue between guests, hosts and chefs is part of the meal.

50minutes
8ingredients
6courses
3levels of dining

Media and web, a movement canvas, a lighting palette, multi-level dining and the kitchen: the event’s elements at a glance.

The working wall

Drawings, notes, textures.

The exhibited panels have not survived, but the wall of working drawings has: sketches of the plated dishes, carrot studies, texture samples and ink drawings, pinned up side by side.

The research

Food, service, atmosphere.

By unfolding the traditional elements that make a holistic dining experience, and understanding their temporality, the experiments seek a new framework for sequenced composition, choreography and effects in design.

Exhibition

Digital compositions.

For the Architecture Class exhibition, digital compositions on the subject of food and architecture were formalised through a video project that brought in music and time, elaborating movement in digital space.
