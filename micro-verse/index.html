Kingspan Micro-Verse

Luxury nomadism in 24.96 m² spheres: an entry to the MICROHOME Kingspan Edition.

2023Year
CompetitionType
P. WarithkorasuthCo-author
Satire · LuxuryTheme

A satirical exploration of luxury nomadism: 24.96 m² spheres of exclusion, Tesla solar roofs, private helipads, and the ethics of compact living for the 1%.

The brief

Under 25 m², off the grid.

The MICROHOME Kingspan Edition, hosted by Buildner with Kingspan, asked for an off-grid modular home for a young professional couple, within 25 m² of floor area, anywhere in the world, with preference for solutions to economic, social and cultural challenges.

The MicroVerse Initiative flips the “tiny home” narrative and reframes the micro-home as a high-end investment vehicle for the ultra-wealthy. Each sphere comes with integrated PV, Skystream turbines and an optional Tesla Powerwall 3, while its social implications are deliberately left unresolved.

The spheres

Five places to disappear to.

Desert

EcoNomad Haven

Terracotta-clad sphere for desert deployment, with passive cooling and a minimal contact footprint on the ground.

Sky

AeroNest Skyward

Sphere lifted on a slim pole above the clouds, with a private helipad, for when the road is optional.

Coast

AquaVista Seafarer

Coastal float unit with tidal anchoring and a panoramic view of the sea.

Earth

EarthScape Dweller

A sphere set into the ground under a planted roof, warm inside and hidden from the outside.

Deep

AquaSphere Explorer

Transparent observation deck below the surface, with a pressurised habitation core.
